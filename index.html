How it works

Up and running in one class

For teachers

  1. Register with your subjectsPick each class and subject you teach, such as BBA 1st Semester — Computer Applications.
  2. Publish a quiz after the lectureMultiple choice or true/false, with an optional timer and close time.
  3. Check the gradebookSee who took it, class averages and final marks.

For students

  1. Create an account and choose your classOnce, with your roll number. You're enrolled in every subject of your class.
  2. Open your dashboardQuizzes from all your subjects appear there as teachers publish them.
  3. Answer and see your scoreReview correct answers when the quiz closes.
