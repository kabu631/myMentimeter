How it works

  1. Add each subject you teach with its class, for example BBA · 1st Semester — Computer Applications.
  2. Students choose their class when they register and are enrolled in all of its subjects automatically. Share the sign-up link from the Students page.
  3. After each class, publish a quiz. Students answer and see their score immediately.
  4. Close the quiz when time is up (or set an automatic close time). Students who missed it score zero.
  5. End of semester: export the Gradebook, then move the class to its next semester from the Students page. Marks already earned stay on every student's report.
